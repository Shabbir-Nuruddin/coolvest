## Platform

web

## Users

- **Buyer (primary):** employers who outfit outdoor or uniformed workforces in hot climates. These are security agencies at premium sites, quick-commerce and delivery fleets, facilities management firms, construction and industrial contractors in India, and Gulf contractors. They buy per worker, in volume, from a welfare or safety budget.
- **Wearer:** the worker who wears it under a uniform for a long shift, such as a security guard, delivery rider, site or factory worker. They are usually not the person who pays.
- **Secondary:** individuals who buy one for themselves.

## Product Purpose

CoolVest is a sleeveless undershirt with removable phase-change-material (PCM) liners. The liners absorb body heat at about 28 °C while the wearer works, and it stays invisible under a normal work uniform. It has no fans, batteries or water. The site exists to turn employer interest into quote requests and pilot orders. Individuals can also register to buy. The owner will share the site publicly only once the product is finished and tested. Until then it is built launch-ready.

## Positioning

It is the only cooling product designed to disappear under a uniform:
- It is a thin undershirt, not a bulky vest.
- It has no electronics.
- The liners are spread out (a back panel plus two chest pads) and sit only where heat is shed. Nothing goes on the stomach, shoulders, neck or arms.
- It is sold as a system: one shirt, two liner sets, and a midday swap.

## Operating Context

- The liners recharge in a fridge or freezer. They do not recharge in ambient Indian heat, hot water or body heat. A wearer needs cold storage between uses, and a site needs a fridge for the midday swap.
- Remove all liner pieces before washing the shirt. The shirt is machine-washable.
- B2B buying runs on quotes, pilots and volume. Procurement wants specs and honesty, not hype.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- **Liner construction:** TPU film, HF-welded. Each cell is about 3.5 cm and individually sealed. There are 4 mm vent holes at the weld intersections.
- **Skin side:** open mesh.
- **Outer side:** a reflective/insulating layer.
- **PCM:** paraffin or bio-based, with a ~28 °C transition. The target is about 480 g, split roughly 75% back / 25% chest (back panel ~35 × 40 cm, chest pads ~18 × 14 cm). These are design targets, not tested figures.
- **Not yet confirmed:**
  - pricing (the site says "request a quote")
  - cooling duration
  - temperature drop
  - wash and cycle life
  - certifications
  - sizing range

## Brand Commitments

- Name: **CoolVest**.
- Voice: plain, specific, honest, and India-first with Gulf reach. Avoid hype words such as "advanced", "revolutionary" or "cutting-edge".
- Avoid tactical, military, sportswear and fashion styling.

## Product Principles

1. Invisible under the uniform is the whole product.
2. Honest physics: it cools only while the PCM is melting, and recharging needs cold.
3. Sell the system to employers (shirt, two liner sets, swap), not a gadget to individuals.
4. No claim without a test behind it.
